## 🚀 What It Is
Waves Mercury for Windows is a big, friendly collection of audio plug-ins that you load straight into a compatible DAW. Think of the Waves Mercury bundle as a well-stocked toolbox: instead of one effect, you get many categories of processors — equalizers, compressors and limiters, reverbs, delays, modulation, and saturation — all appearing in your host's effects list. Whether you're mixing a song, mastering a final track, or designing new sounds, mercury waves tools give you plenty of options without ever leaving your project.

> Everything installs as plug-ins in your DAW, so you can insert, tweak, automate, and recall them just like any other effect you already use.

## ✨ Key Features
- 🎛️ **Lots of processor types** — EQ, dynamics, reverb, delay, and more gathered into one bundle.
- 🔊 **Dynamics that deliver** — compressors, limiters, and gates to control level and add energy.
- 🎼 **Tone shaping made easy** — musical and precise EQ options for corrective and creative work.
- 🌊 **Space and depth** — reverbs and delays that add ambience and dimension to any mix.
- 🔌 **Loads right in** — each processor shows up as a plug-in in your DAW, ready to go.
